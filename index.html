Chroma Drift

The world has lost its colours. Shoot down the creatures, collect the colour drops they leave behind and fill the three meters to paint the landscape back to life.

The ball bounces on its own and you only control its spin – until you earn better powers. Green gems move the cursor along the power bar; activate when the right power is lit.
